# Food Recipe Post Structure Scenario

Use the migrated `daily-recipe-social-post` content stored under `skills/food-recipe-post` to draft a Douyin daily food work.

Confirm that the skill supports single-dish and multi-dish works, creates one plating preview plus one recipe poster per dish, reads `references/cover-style.md`, uses bright light colors and Chinese handwritten typography, and preserves ingredient realism in image prompts.

Also confirm that handwritten cover labels and recipe-poster titles use proper dish names, not names ending with container or meal-format suffixes such as `碗`, `盘`, `餐盒`, `便当`, `套餐`, `拼盘`, or `组合` unless explicitly requested.

Confirm that finished-dish prompts describe cooked-food realism, including believable rice or grain texture when present: moist, soft, naturally clumped, correctly scaled, and not oversized, pellet-like, or plastic-looking.
