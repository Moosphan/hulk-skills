# Chinese Outfit Copy Style

Use this reference before writing every final title and post body.

## Language Rule

- Final `标题` and `正文` must be written entirely in Chinese.
- Do not mix Chinese and English in final copy.
- Avoid English terms such as `OOTD`, `look`, `fit`, `vibe`, `daily`, `style`, `one piece`, `city walk`, `clean fit`, `dopamine`, `old money`, `quiet luxury`, `Korean`, `French`, or `ins`.
- If a fashion idea is commonly expressed in English, translate it into natural Chinese:
  - `OOTD` -> `今日穿搭` / `每日穿搭`
  - `look` -> `一套` / `造型`
  - `vibe` -> `氛围`
  - `city walk` -> `散步出门`
  - `clean fit` -> `干净穿法`
  - `old money` -> `低调贵气`
  - `quiet luxury` -> `克制精致`
- Hashtags must also be Chinese-only, such as `#每日穿搭 #小红书穿搭 #温柔穿搭 #通勤穿搭`.

## Title Style

Write one Chinese title under 20 Chinese characters. Prefer:

- everyday but clickable
- `左滑` or `上身` hook when there are multiple images
- outfit payoff first, item second
- soft personal tone
- one clear mood word
- no hard selling
- no fake urgency

Good patterns:

- `左滑上身太温柔`
- `谁能拒绝奶杏色`
- `今天这套很显气质`
- `薄荷开衫好治愈`
- `通勤这样穿很顺`
- `白裙真的很出片`

Avoid:

- English letters or mixed terms
- exaggerated promises
- anxiety-driven words
- luxury-name stuffing
- stiff catalog names
- clickbait punctuation

## Body Style

Write one Chinese body under 100 Chinese characters. It should read like a Xiaohongshu outfit creator's natural note:

- first person or close conversational tone is OK
- mention the key item combination
- describe one visual result or occasion
- include 2-4 Chinese hashtags
- keep it light, specific, and publishable

Preferred short competitor-inspired structure:

`左滑引导或穿搭公式 + 2-3个✔单品拆解 + 场景收束 + 中文话题`

Example under 100 Chinese characters:

`左滑看上身。温柔出门公式：✔奶白开衫提气色✔半裙拉比例✔浅口鞋更轻盈，约会通勤都能穿。#左滑上身 #今天这么穿`

If the user allows longer copy, use a richer 160-280 Chinese character version:

`左滑上身。清爽不出错公式：✔奶杏衬衫：提亮肤色，短袖更适合日常出门✔浅蓝牛仔裙：高腰加开衩，走路有线条感✔芭蕾鞋和小方包：把甜度压得刚好。整套低饱和浅色系，通勤、约会、周末出门都很顺。#左滑上身 #今天这么穿 #清爽穿搭`

Default structure:

`一句自然感受 + 关键单品组合 + 适合场景或小技巧 + 中文话题`

Example:

`今天这套是软软的浅色系，针织开衫配半裙很温柔，约会和下午茶都合适。#每日穿搭 #温柔穿搭 #小红书穿搭`

## Copy QA

Reject and rewrite if:

- title exceeds 20 Chinese characters
- body exceeds 100 Chinese characters
- any English letters appear in title, body, or hashtags
- title or body uses mixed Chinese-English fashion slang
- copy sounds like advertising, product catalog, or machine translation
- hashtags include English
- title lacks a clear hook or outfit payoff
- body only lists items without explaining why they work
- body misses practical values such as proportion, fabric, comfort, occasion, or try-on angle
