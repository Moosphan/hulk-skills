# Bundled Open-Source Fonts

These fonts are used for poem-fill covers and are included to keep the skill portable.
License texts are stored in `licenses/`.

| Preset | Typeface | Recommended use | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `zcool-xiaowei` | ZCOOL XiaoWei | Default; poised classical display text | Google Fonts |
| `lxgw-wenkai` | LXGW WenKai | Gentle literary and handwritten mood | LXGW WenKai project |
| `mashanzheng` | Ma Shan Zheng | Restrained calligraphic accents | Google Fonts |

All bundled typefaces use the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The former `shangwei` and
`zcool-qingke` presets are deliberately excluded from this skill and must not be
selected for new covers.
